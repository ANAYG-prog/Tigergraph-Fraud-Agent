I built an agentic fraud investigator on @TigerGraphDB for the #HHGOA hackathon 🕵️

It takes an uncertain fraud signal and turns it into a defensible action:
🔗 It walks the graph through TigerGraph MCP: shared devices, multi-hop rings, WCC communities, and prior cases linked in the graph
📚 GraphRAG over the bank's policy and fraud typologies grounds every recommendation
🧠 Case memory: closed investigations calibrate the risk model and supply precedent
❓ It asks for extra evidence only when that evidence is likely to change the decision (value of information), and records the next best action before and after
🛡️ Policy-gated actions with approval routes, SAR drafts, and a full audit trail
